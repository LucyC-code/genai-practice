---
name: Notre Dame event sources
description: Reliable public sources and verification pitfalls for University of Notre Dame campus-event research.
---

Use https://nd.campuslabs.com/engage/events for student club events and https://events.nd.edu/ and https://sao.nd.edu/events for university and student activities events. NDCentral event detail pages expose dates, times, locations, descriptions, and host organizations publicly even when RSVP requires login.

**Why:** General Instagram searches mixed unrelated schools named Notre Dame with old announcements. Notre Dame's university-wide calendar also lists events in downtown South Bend, which are not on campus.

**How to apply:** Check each primary event page and campus location. Use the university's club directory at https://msps.nd.edu/get-involved/student-organizations to verify club Instagram accounts. Treat an Instagram caption containing "tomorrow" or "Thursday" as undated unless the original posting date can be verified; do not assume the calendar year from a search result.

NDCentral's public RSS feed at https://nd.campuslabs.com/engage/events.rss exposes more listings than the first page of the event directory, including descriptions, hosts, locations, status, and offset-aware start/end dates. RSS publication dates can be the feed generation time, not event or original announcement dates.

**Why:** Directory results were limited to the first 15 cards, while the public feed revealed hundreds of listings. Several club descriptions contradicted their structured schedule, so copying a description's time without cross-checking would mislead users.

**How to apply:** Use the structured event start/end metadata for discovery, cross-check description times and cancellation status, and exclude or explicitly flag conflicts. Do not treat a graduate presenter or a class council host alone as proof of attendee eligibility.
